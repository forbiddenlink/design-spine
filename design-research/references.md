- Land-book entries returned browser HTTP 403 challenge screens. Listing information was readable with web tools; actual pen.dev, Haptic, and Exemplar sites loaded HTTP 200. Ferio entry was blocked and excluded; no fabricated live URL.
- Awwwards web-fetch entries failed, but genuine browser entry pages loaded HTTP 200, stated SOTD and dates, and exposed live destination links. Entry screenshots and `award-*.json` preserve that evidence.
- An initial guessed Exemplar subdomain failed certificate validation. Replaced with the publisher-confirmed exemplarfromsweden.com; no TLS bypass.

## Direction chosen from evidence

An editorial review desk: warm paper, near-black ink, a precise orange index mark, monospace capture metadata, image-led cards, and clear controls. Visual Society's indexing, Boc's accent restraint, Butter's simple controls, and pen.dev's visible product inform the direction. Strong brand framing should make the review process recognizable without competing with the captured page or falsely implying a capture passed.
